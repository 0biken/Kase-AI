02 — Logo

Logo
System

The Kase logomark is a geometric K monogram that integrates a magnifying lens into its negative space — representing the core product action: investigation and audit. The mark is precision-engineered, not decorative.

Logo System 2 × 3 Grid · Board 02
VariantUsageMinimum Size
Primary LockupK icon + "Kase" wordmark — marketing, website, documentation80px wide
Icon MarkK monogram only — app icons, favicons, small-format16 × 16px
Badge / SealCircular K mark — avatars, social profiles, CI/CD bot icons24 × 24px
Wordmark Only"Kase" text — inline references, small UI labels48px wide

✓ Do

  • Maintain minimum clear space (1× icon height)
  • Use on high-contrast backgrounds
  • Keep proportions exactly as specified
  • Use approved color variants only

✗ Don't

  • Stretch, skew, or rotate the logo
  • Add drop shadows, glows, or effects
  • Place on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Modify the mark geometry in any way
